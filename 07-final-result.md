# Final Result — The Last Train

## Final Video

**The Last Train**

A short-form cinematic AI video created through a
shot-by-shot generative video workflow.

### Story

A man waits alone at an almost-empty metro station late at
night.

After receiving a mysterious notification, he looks around
and sees a person who looks exactly like him.

The two characters stare at each other while a metro train
passes along the side of the platform.

The video ends with a final message:

> **I TOLD YOU NOT TO LOOK.**

## Production Summary

The project demonstrates a practical workflow for:

- AI-assisted storytelling
- Character reference creation
- Shot planning
- Prompt-based video generation
- Character consistency
- Visual quality control
- Iterative problem solving
- Post-production
- Sound design collaboration

## Final Workflow

**Story**
→ **Character Reference**
→ **Shot Design**
→ **AI Generation**
→ **Quality Check**
→ **Iteration**
→ **Visual Edit**
→ **Sound Design**
→ **Final Video**

## Key Takeaway

The main focus of this project was not simply generating an
AI video.

It was experimenting with how a creative idea can be broken
into controllable production steps, how AI-generation problems
can be identified, and how the workflow can be adjusted when
the model does not produce the intended result.

## Collaboration

Sound design was completed collaboratively with a sound
engineer, who developed the audio direction and final sound
layering for the video.
