# Production Brief — The Last Train

## Objective

Create a short-form cinematic AI video that demonstrates
consistent character generation, controlled shot design,
prompt iteration, and practical AI video production.

## Format

- Vertical video
- 9:16 aspect ratio
- Short-form narrative
- Psychological thriller / mystery

## Creative Direction

The video should feel:

- Cinematic
- Photorealistic
- Atmospheric
- Minimal
- Suspenseful
- Set in a realistic metro environment at night

The tension should come from the situation, camera framing,
character reactions, and sound rather than exaggerated horror
effects.

## Story Premise

A man waits alone at an almost-empty metro station late at
night.

His phone vibrates and he receives a mysterious warning:

> **DON'T LOOK BACK.**

He looks around and sees another man who looks exactly like him.

The two characters stare at each other while a metro train
passes along the side of the platform.

The video ends with another mysterious message:

> **I TOLD YOU NOT TO LOOK.**

## Production Requirements

The workflow needed to maintain:

- Character identity
- Clothing consistency
- Environment continuity
- 9:16 framing
- Consistent nighttime lighting
- Cinematic visual language
- Controlled character movement
- Continuity between shots

## Main AI Challenges

The main challenges encountered during generation were:

1. Complex hand and phone interactions
2. Character and clothing consistency
3. Spatial continuity within the metro environment
4. Maintaining the correct vertical framing
5. Reliable text generation on phone screens
6. Natural character movement

These limitations influenced how the shots were designed,
generated, reviewed, and iterated.

## Production Approach

The story was divided into individual shots rather than
attempting to generate the complete sequence in one prompt.

Each shot was generated and reviewed separately before moving
to the next stage.

The overall workflow was:

**Story → Character Reference → Shot Design → AI Generation → Review → Iteration → Editing → Sound Design**
