# Quality Control — The Last Train

Every generated shot was reviewed before being added to the
final sequence.

The purpose of the review was to catch visual problems early
rather than allowing errors to carry into later shots.

## Character Consistency

Checked for:

- Same facial identity
- Same hairstyle
- Same clothing
- Consistent body proportions
- Consistent overall appearance

## Environment Consistency

Checked for:

- Same metro-station setting
- Consistent nighttime atmosphere
- Similar lighting
- Consistent visual style
- No unexpected environmental changes

## Camera & Composition

Checked for:

- Correct 9:16 vertical framing
- No unexpected switch to 16:9
- Stable camera movement
- Appropriate framing for the story beat
- No unwanted camera jumps

## Motion Quality

Checked for:

- Natural body movement
- Correct hand movement
- Stable objects
- No duplicated objects
- No extra limbs or fingers
- No unnatural character movement

## Narrative Continuity

Checked whether:

- The action follows naturally from the previous shot
- Character positions make sense
- The phone remains consistent
- The visual information matches the story
- Each shot communicates its intended purpose

## Text & Graphics

AI-generated text was not treated as final.

Important text was added during post-production to ensure that
the wording and placement were accurate.

## Final Review

Before export, the complete sequence was watched as one video
rather than reviewing the clips only as individual generations.

The final review focused on whether the individual shots felt
like one continuous story.
